Every garage door spring has a number: the count of open and close cycles it is built to survive before the steel fatigues and it snaps. Standard residential springs are rated for about 10,000. High-cycle springs are rated for 25,000, and some for 50,000. In Florida, where the garage is the front door, the number matters more than in most of the country. Here is how to decide.

![A broken torsion spring on a garage door, snapped at the coil with a visible gap](/images/broken-torsion-spring.webp)

## What makes a spring high-cycle

Thicker wire, more coils, greater length. The same energy is stored across more material, so each coil works further inside its fatigue limit and lasts longer.

It is not a coating or a treatment. It is a different physical size, which is why it needs room on the torsion tube and why we measure before we promise one will fit.

## Why Florida doors cycle hard

In most of the country a garage door opens when a car leaves and closes when it comes back. In Florida the garage is the storage room, the workshop, the laundry, the place the bikes and the golf cart live, and the door most families actually walk through. Nobody uses the front door.

Count it for a week. Two drivers leaving and returning is four cycles. Add the school run, the trash, the dog, a delivery, kids in and out of the garage fridge, and you are at eight to ten without noticing. In golf cart communities like The Villages and the 55 plus developments around Boynton Beach and Pembroke Pines, the cart goes out twice a day on top of the car. Vacation rental homes around Kissimmee see every guest treat the garage door as the front door.

One cycle is one full open plus one full close. At 10,000 cycles, a door used eight times a day gets about three and a half years out of a standard spring.

## The arithmetic

| Cycles per day | Cycles per year | 10,000 cycle spring | 25,000 cycle spring |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 730 | About 13.7 years | About 34 years |
| 4 | 1,460 | About 6.8 years | About 17 years |
| 6 | 2,190 | About 4.6 years | About 11.4 years |
| 8 | 2,920 | About 3.4 years | About 8.6 years |
| 10 | 3,650 | About 2.7 years | About 6.8 years |

Now put the visit next to it. [Spring replacement](/services/garage-door-spring-replacement/) is $150 to $350 per spring installed, and both springs together adds $120 to $250 for the second. Most of that price is the visit, the winding and the balance check, not the spring. The high-cycle spring moves you toward the top of the same range.

Take the eight cycle a day family over ten years.

**Standard springs.** New pair at year zero, replaced at about year three and a half, and again at about year seven. That is two service visits in ten years, each one $270 to $600 for the pair.

**High-cycle springs.** New pair at year zero, replaced once at about year eight and a half. One visit.

The high-cycle premium is the difference between the bottom and the top of one price range, paid once. The visit it saves costs $270 to $600 for the pair. And a standard spring does not pick a convenient day to break. If it goes at 6:30 on a weekday morning with the car inside, the same-day call-out adds an emergency premium of $150 to $300 on top of the repair.

On a busy door, this is straightforward arithmetic.

## Who should buy them

**Doors used six or more times a day.** Any household where the garage is the way in and out. In newer subdivisions across Wesley Chapel, Riverview, Lake Nona, Winter Garden and Port St. Lucie, where the first round of builder springs is failing right now, this is the moment to upgrade.

**Golf cart and 55 plus communities.** Several cycles a day, every day, year round.

**Heavy doors.** Wood, impact-rated glass, and the heavier hurricane-rated steel doors common in [Miami-Dade County](/service-areas/miami-dade-county/) and [Broward County](/service-areas/broward-county/) work their springs harder. Our guide on [how much a garage door weighs](/blog/how-much-does-a-garage-door-weigh/) explains why.

**Doors you plan to keep.** If the door has fifteen years left in it, a spring that lasts nine makes sense.

**Seasonal homes, for a different reason.** A Naples or Bonita Springs house used six months a year does not cycle much, but the owner finds the failure in November, when everyone else does too. A longer rated spring means fewer surprises on the return.

## Who should not

If your door runs twice a day, a standard spring lasts around thirteen years and the premium buys you very little.

If headroom on the torsion tube is genuinely tight, the physical size may rule it out. Low headroom single door garages from the 1950s to 1970s are where this comes up.

If the door is near the end of its life, spend the money on the [new door](/blog/repair-or-replace-garage-door/) instead of on springs that will outlive it.

## Salt air changes the number

A cycle rating assumes clean steel. Corrosion at the coils creates stress points, and a spring near the coast can fail well short of its rating. Within a few miles of either coast, ask for a galvanized or coated spring in whatever cycle rating you choose, and lubricate it three times a year. Our guide on [how long garage door springs last in Florida](/blog/how-long-do-garage-door-springs-last/) goes through everything that shortens the count.

## What to ask for

When you get a spring quote, ask for three things: the wire size, the inside diameter and length, and the cycle rating.

A company that gives you all three is a company that measured your door. A company that cannot say what rating it is fitting is fitting whatever was on the van, and you have no way to compare that quote to any other.
