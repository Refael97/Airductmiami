## Why the price range is so wide

Getting a door back on its track is not the expensive part. The expensive part is whatever got bent in the process.

A door that jumped the track and was left alone is usually an hour of work: release the tension, re-seat the rollers, check the track, re-tension, test. That sits at the bottom of the range.

A door that came off and was then run with the opener several more times is a different job. Track gets bent, rollers get destroyed, the bottom section gets creased, and the cable that probably caused it in the first place is now wrapped around the drum.

## What it costs

| Situation | Price |
|---|---|
| Clean derailment, nothing bent | $150 to $300 |
| Derailment with roller and track damage | $300 to $500 |
| Derailment with cable and drum damage | $400 to $700 |
| Severe, with section replacement | $700 and up |

## The single most useful thing you can do

Stop pressing the button.

Every additional cycle after a door comes off the track converts a straightforward repair into an expensive one. The opener does not know the door is off; it will keep pulling until something gives.

Pull the manual release cord, leave the door where it is, and call someone. If the door is stuck open and the house is exposed, say so when you call, because that is a genuine emergency and it gets treated as one.

## What causes it

**A broken cable.** One side loses support, the door lifts crooked and the rollers on the low side pop out. This is the most common cause we see.

**Impact.** A vehicle bumping the door, even lightly, knocks the track out of alignment. The door may run for weeks afterwards before it derails.

**Worn or seized rollers.** A roller that will not turn drags in the track. Over months it wears the track and eventually climbs out of it.

**Track alignment.** Loose track brackets let the track spread under load. If your door has been noisy and rough for a while, this is often why.

## What should happen afterwards

Re-seating the door is the middle of the job, not the end of it. The visit should finish with the cause identified and dealt with, the track checked along its full length, the rollers inspected, the cables checked on both sides, and a balance test with the opener disconnected.

If nobody tells you why the door came off, it will come off again.
