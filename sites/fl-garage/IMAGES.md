# Homepage imagery

Four generated illustrations. Per `docs/MEDIA-STANDARD.md` these may
illustrate and may not impersonate: they show the kinds of door common to
each part of the metro area, and the homepage says so in a caption
underneath. None of them is presented as our own work.

The build environment cannot reach the generation CDN, so these files are
uploaded to the repository by hand rather than fetched. Until they are
present the page still lays out correctly: every image has a reserved 4:3
box and the emergency band falls back to the flat navy gradient.

| File | Used by | Source prompt |
|---|---|---|
| `ny-colonial-double-door.webp` | Emergency band background, behind a heavy navy wash | Long Island colonial, charcoal insulated steel double door, autumn, overcast |
| `brooklyn-under-house-single-door.webp` | Doors band, card 1 | South Brooklyn attached brick, narrow single under the living space, low headroom |
| `westchester-carriage-doors.webp` | Doors band, card 2 | Westchester shingle and stone colonial, detached three-bay carriage house doors in stained timber |
| `long-island-split-level-door.webp` | Doors band, card 3 | 1960s Long Island split level, white flush panel steel single door |

Generation rules that were applied, from the media standard: region
specific rather than generic American housing, no people, no vehicles, no
text, no signage, no logos, no house numbers, and a palette that sits with
the navy and slate of the brand.

## Replacing these with real photographs

The moment a real job produces before and after photos, they do not go
here. They go in a gallery of their own, labelled with the city and the
service, and the caption under the doors band is updated to point at it.
Real work photography outperforms anything generated, and mixing the two
under one caption is how a site loses the right to claim either.
