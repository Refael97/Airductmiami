## Precision Garage Door (`precisiondoor.net`)

National franchise, and **the strongest content architecture in this
vertical**. Worth studying more than any local competitor.

### The symptom-page cluster

They do not write one "troubleshooting" page. They write **one page per
specific symptom**, at a granularity almost nobody matches:

- `/garage-door-wont-go-down` — "12 Reasons Why Your Garage Door Won't
  Close (& What To Do)"
- `/garage-door-starts-to-close-then-stops` — a *separate* page for a
  *variant* of the same symptom
- `/garage-door-sensors` — component-level guide
- `/how-long-do-garage-door-springs-last`
- `/faq`

Two things to steal:

1. **Splitting near-identical symptoms into separate pages.** "Won't close"
   and "starts to close then reverses" are the same component (the photo
   eyes) but different searches by different people. Each gets a page.
2. **Numbered listicle titles**: "12 Reasons Why X (& What To Do)". A count
   plus a promise of action. This is the pattern that wins symptom SERPs.

### Their page recipe (inferred from titles and SERP snippets)

Answer up top → numbered causes ordered by likelihood (misaligned photo
eyes first, because it is the most common and the most fixable) → simple
DIY checks the reader can do now → the line where DIY stops → call CTA.

**The tactic underneath**: give away the free fixes generously. The reader
who solves it themselves trusts you; the reader who cannot has already
decided who to call. Site one used the same logic with the scam article.

### What to do differently

They still do not publish prices on the symptom pages. Our version answers
"what will this cost me?" inside the symptom page, which is the question the
reader actually has while reading about their broken door.

## All Door & Garage (`alldoorandgarage.com`), Elmsford NY

The closest thing to a direct regional competitor found so far, and a useful
reality check.

| | |
|---|---|
| Since | 2008, 16+ years |
| Base | Elmsford, Westchester |
| Coverage | **45+ named towns**: Westchester, Rockland, the 5 boroughs, plus New Jersey and Connecticut |
| Rating | 4.9 with 16 reviews, BBB A+ |
| Memberships | International Door Association |
| Scope | Residential + commercial, roll-up gates, loading docks, metal entry doors |
| Offers | 10% senior discount, 10% off new installations |

### What this tells us

1. **The Westchester and Rockland corridor is real and under-served.** Their
   town list (Scarsdale, Rye, Larchmont, Bronxville, Chappaqua, Armonk,
   White Plains, New Rochelle, Yonkers, Tarrytown) is high-income,
   garage-dense, and matches our tier 5. It should probably move up.
2. **16 reviews is a beatable number.** These are not review fortresses. A
   real review engine catches them inside a year.
3. **Commercial is a live segment** here: roll-up gates, loading docks,
   metal entry doors. Very different buyer, much bigger contract, worth at
   least one page and a decision later.
4. **Discount offers are the norm** (senior, new install). Our popup
   discount framing fits the market convention exactly.
5. Even a real, established, well-reviewed company with a 45-town list
   **still does not publish prices**. The lane stays open.
