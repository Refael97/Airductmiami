# Florida Breeze: what Search Console actually says, 7 September 2026

Pulled live from Google Search Console for `sc-domain:floridabreezeairduct.com`,
final data, 8 June to 4 September 2026. Every number here is measured, not
estimated. Where something is inferred it says so.

This document exists because the expansion request was "more pages, more
Spanish, more services, more areas". The data agrees with three of those and
disagrees with one, and the disagreement is worth more than the agreement.

## 1. The finding that decides the plan

| | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Pages with impressions | 166 | 120 |
| Impressions | 25,841 | 3,955 |
| Clicks | 63 | 61 |
| CTR | 0.24% | 1.54% |
| Average position | 32.4 | 13.3 |

**Spanish earns the same number of clicks from 15% of the impressions.**
It ranks 19 positions higher and converts at 6.4 times the rate.

The English side is not short of pages. It has 166 of them, they pull 25,841
impressions, and they produce 63 clicks, because position 32 is the second
and third page of results. Adding more English pages adds more impressions at
position 32. It does not add clicks.

So "more pages" is the wrong lever, and it is worth saying that plainly before
building anything. The right levers are Spanish depth, and depth on the
English pages that already have impressions.

in Deerfield Beach, Bradenton, Winter Park and Plantation.

That is the useful lesson. The Spanish city list was copied from the English
one. It should follow Hispanic population density instead. The cities where
Spanish already wins are Kissimmee, Orlando, Tampa, Kendall, West Palm Beach,
Hialeah, Miami. The expansion belongs there and in the cities like them that
have no page yet.

## 4. Mold: real demand, no ranking, and a licensing question

158 mold related queries, **516 impressions, zero clicks, average position
64.4**.

| Query | Impressions | Position |
|---|---|---|
| air duct mold removal | 96 | 89.6 |
| air duct mold removal cost palm beach, fl | 48 | 48.1 |
| mold removal from air ducts | 18 | 73.9 |
| air duct cleaning for allergies and mold remediation hollywood fl | 17 | 13.7 |
| air duct mold removal cost | 17 | 89.2 |
| mold in vents | 12 | 49.6 |
| black mold in air ducts | 10 | 60.6 |

The site has had a `mold-remediation` service page since the build, and it was
renamed to "Air Duct Mold Removal" on 15 July specifically to chase this. Two
months later the head query moved from position 96 to 89.6. It is not working.

In Spanish the same demand is tiny but the pages rank: three `moho` queries,
and `/es/blog/moho-en-ductos-aire/` sits at position 8.6 with 165 impressions.

**Before any of this is expanded there is a legal question to settle.** Florida
licenses mold assessors and mold remediators under Chapter 468 Part XVI. A
separate research pass is running on whether this company may lawfully
advertise "mold remediation" and "mold removal" at all, and that answer gates
the entire cluster. Nothing in the mold cluster ships until it lands.

## 5. Sliding glass doors

Zero impressions. Not a single query containing "sliding" reached this site in
90 days, which is expected: there is no content, so there is no data. This is
a cold vertical and Search Console cannot say anything about it. A separate
research pass is establishing whether the demand and the competition justify
it, and whether it belongs on an air duct site at all or wants its own.

## 6. What this means for the expansion

In priority order, by measured impressions at stake.

**First, fix what already has demand.** 4,421 impressions sitting at position
21 to 40 with zero clicks, plus 1,526 at position 5 to 20. This is depth on
existing pages: Deerfield Beach, Bradenton, Hollywood, Sunrise, Fort
Lauderdale, Boynton Beach, Clearwater, Pembroke Pines, Plantation. No new URLs
required. The city pages that rank are the ones with real neighbourhood level
detail, which is what `cityDetail.ts` was built for and it currently covers
only a handful of cities.

**Second, Spanish where Spanish speakers are.** Not a copy of the English city
list. Kissimmee, Orlando, Tampa, Kendall, Hialeah, Miami, West Palm Beach and
the cities like them, with the Spanish cost and near me queries targeted
directly. The one Spanish query that already earns every Spanish click is at
position 10.4 and belongs at position 3.

**Third, mold, once the licensing answer is in.** 516 impressions, position
64, and a service page that two months of work has not moved.

**Fourth, sliding doors, only if the research says the demand is real and the
adjacency does not damage the duct site's topical focus.**

What I would not do is add English city pages. The site has 38 of them, they
carry 25,841 impressions, and they return 63 clicks. The constraint is
position, not page count.
