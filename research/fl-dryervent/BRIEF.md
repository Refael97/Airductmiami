# Dryer Vent Florida: research brief

Site id `fl-dryervent`, domain dryerventflorida.com, bought by the owner on
21 October 2026. Third site in the network, second in the duct and vent trade.

## Why this site exists

Florida Breeze Air Duct (fl-airduct) is an air duct site that also cleans
dryer vents. Over 1 July to 19 October 2026 its Search Console shows 446
distinct dryer vent queries, **11,344 impressions, 6,994 of them on phones,
and one click**. Google shows its air duct and city pages for dryer vent
searches, ranks them around 10 to 48, and searchers pass them over because
the result is about something else.

A site that is only about dryer vents answers those searches directly. It
does not compete with Florida Breeze for its own head terms ("air duct
cleaning ..."), because it does not target them.

## Services

1. Dryer vent cleaning, $100 to $200
2. Dryer vent repair, $200 to $600
3. Dryer vent installation and replacement (rigid metal, rerouting, new
   laundry locations), $200 to $600
4. Roof and wall vent caps: bird nests, stuck dampers, missing guards,
   priced inside the cleaning or repair range
5. Condo, apartment and multi-unit buildings: priced per unit, quoted per
   building

Prices are identical to Florida Breeze's published dryer vent prices. The
same crews do the work, and the network rule is that no two pages anywhere
contradict each other on price.

## Positioning

The specialist. One trade, explained properly: airflow measured before and
after, rigid metal duct, the Florida Building Code length rule (35 ft less
2.5 ft per 45 degree and 5 ft per 90 degree fitting unless the dryer maker
allows more), no screws into the duct, no foil hose in walls, a damper that
closes. Fire risk stated with the US Fire Administration's figures, not
invented ones.

## Rules carried from the network

- No links between this site and Florida Breeze or Garage Door Fixers.
- No text copied from Florida Breeze: every page written fresh. Google
  treats cross-site duplication from one owner as a doorway network.
- No reviews, ratings or job counts until real ones exist.
- No "licensed" claim: dryer vent cleaning is not a licensed trade in
  Florida. "Insured" is true.
- Own GA4 property and Search Console property; nothing shared with
  fl-airduct's analytics.
