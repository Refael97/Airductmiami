# Referral terms

Tomas at 911 Restoration opened at 5 percent, contingent on him winning the
work.

## Assessment

5 percent is the floor of the normal referral range, not an insult. It is
also an opening offer, and it is low relative to what transfers with the
introduction: a live federal requirement, the scope document, a named
government point of contact, a site visit already scheduled, and an
established relationship with the prime. Marketing cost to him is zero and
bid hunting cost is zero. A contractor chasing federal work pays more than
this for less.

Counter at 10. Expect to land at 7.5.

## But the percentage is not where the money is

On a 40,000 USD job:

| Rate | Fee |
|---|---|
| 5 percent | 2,000 |
| 7.5 percent | 3,000 |
| 10 percent | 4,000 |

The spread between the opening offer and the ceiling is 2,000 USD.

MacDill is a large installation. A contractor who gets base credentials,
completes Building 861 cleanly and leaves JCSE satisfied gets called for the
next building, and for other units on the same base, because facility
managers talk to each other. None of that pays us anything unless the
continuation clause is written now.

**The continuation clause is worth more than the rate.** Spend the
negotiating energy there.

## Four terms that must be defined

**1. Continuation.** The fee applies to any work performed for GovCrest, or
at any facility on MacDill AFB, for 24 months. Not only this contract.

**2. Percentage of precisely what.** A bare percentage is meaningless without
a base. Define it as a percentage of the total amount invoiced to the
customer, **including change orders, scope modifications and any option
periods the government exercises.** Without that wording, a 40,000 USD award
followed by 25,000 USD of change orders pays us on the smaller number. Given
that section 1.0 pushes site condition validation to post award, change
orders on this job are likely rather than hypothetical.

**3. Payment trigger.** Federal payment is slow and typically runs net 30
from invoice. Define it as payable within 15 days of the contractor receiving
payment from the customer. Not "on completion" and not "when the job wraps
up".

**4. What happens if he does not win.** No fee, which is fair. But the
agreement must not tie us to a transaction that never happened. We stay free
to take the opportunity to another contractor if his bid fails or is
withdrawn.

(727) 539-7000, today.

Not as a manoeuvre. So that "I am also speaking with another contractor" is
a true statement when it is said. Saying it without having made the call
means opening a business relationship with a lie, which is a bad trade for a
few hundred dollars.

There is also a substantive reason. Service-Tech holds GSA Federal Supply
Schedule GS-21F-0032U and roughly 60 years in government facilities. Past
performance and capability statement are exactly where the gap between the
two firms is widest, and those are two of the three mandatory evaluation
documents. **5 percent of a job that is won beats 10 percent of a bid that
loses.**

## The rule that does not move

Terms by email before naming the base, the building, GovCrest or Luis.

A phone agreement with someone personable is worth nothing on the day there
is a disagreement. If Tomas is straight, and there is no reason to think
otherwise, a five line email costs him nothing. A counterparty who will not
put in writing what he just said on the phone has told us something.

Either send this and ask him to reply confirming, or ask him to send it.
Either direction works as long as both sides have it in writing.

Subject: `Referral terms, Tampa federal HVAC cleaning requirement`

Tomas,

Confirming what we agreed by phone.

1. Florida Breeze Air Duct is introducing 911 Restoration to a federal HVAC and air conveyance cleaning requirement, including the statement of work, the prime contractor contact and the government point of contact.

2. 911 Restoration will pay Florida Breeze Air Duct a referral fee of [7.5] percent of the total amount invoiced to the customer for this work, including change orders, scope modifications and any option periods exercised by the government.

3. The same fee applies to any further work 911 Restoration performs for this prime contractor, or at this installation, for 24 months from the date of this email.

4. The fee is payable within 15 days of 911 Restoration receiving payment from the customer, for each payment received.

5. No fee is due if 911 Restoration does not win the work. In that case Florida Breeze Air Duct is free to introduce the requirement to another contractor.

6. 911 Restoration is the performing and contracting party. Florida Breeze Air Duct is not a party to the contract, does not perform work and carries no contractual obligation to the customer.

Please reply to confirm and I will send the full package today.

[NAME]
Florida Breeze Air Duct
info@floridabreezeairduct.com

## Note

At this deal size a clear email exchange is proportionate and is normally
enforceable. A drafted contract is not worth the delay with a site visit two
days out. If the continuation clause starts producing repeat work, that is
the point to have a lawyer paper the relationship properly.

Point 6 is not boilerplate. It is the sentence that keeps us out of the
liability chain in sections 4.4, 5.2 and 5.4 of the statement of work. Do not
drop it to make the email shorter.
