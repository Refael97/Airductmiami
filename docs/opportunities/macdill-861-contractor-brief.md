### Scope

Six complete air systems.

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Air Handling Units | 4 |
| Rooftop Units | 2 |
| Supply ductwork systems | 6 |
| Return ductwork systems | 6 |
| Diffuser and grille systems | 6 |
| VAV boxes | Named in scope, no count given |

Work required:

- **Air handling equipment.** Heating and cooling coils, fan blades, housings and plenum walls washed. Fiberglass linings vacuumed.
- **Ductwork.** All accessible interior vacuumed. Access openings cut where needed, closed with sheet metal panels and sealed against leakage. Inaccessible runs cleaned by air pressure technique. **Areas blocked by plaster ceilings, fire walls and masonry are explicitly excluded from contractor responsibility.**
- **Dampers and louvers.** Cleaned and reset to previous positions.
- **Diffusers and grilles.** Readily removable units washed and reinstalled. Riveted or plastered units are out of scope.
- **Filters.** Customer supplies disposable filters, contractor installs. Metal filters washed.
- **Ceiling.** Surfaces around diffusers, registers and grilles vacuumed.
- **Fungistat and bacteriostat.** Applied after cleaning on customer request, per manufacturer specification.
- **Negative air.** HEPA rated vacuum system maintaining negative pressure in the duct system during cleaning where necessary.
- **Protection.** Drop cloths and coverings, all work areas left broom clean.

Customer provides water, electricity and restroom facilities.

Standard is stated as "industry standard". **NADCA is not named anywhere in
the document**, and NADCA certification is not listed as a requirement. I have
asked whether it is scored in the evaluation.
