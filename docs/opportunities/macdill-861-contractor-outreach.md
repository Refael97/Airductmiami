# Contractor outreach, MacDill Building 861

Goal: appoint one Tampa area NADCA commercial contractor to price and perform
this job, with Florida Breeze Air Duct as the party that sourced it and holds
a margin.

Do not send the SOW as an attachment on first contact. Describe it, confirm
they qualify, then send it.

---

## Target list, in order

| Firm | Why first | Known gap |
|---|---|---|
| Service-Tech Corporation | Large commercial and industrial duct cleaning, multi state, handles institutional work | No advertised military installation experience |
| EnviroAir Systems | Tampa area commercial HVAC hygiene | No advertised military installation experience |

Neither publicly advertises work on a military installation, so that is the
opening question, not an assumption. If both come back without base
experience, widen the search to firms that hold an active SAM.gov
registration, which is a fast proxy for having done federal work before.

## Qualifying questions, ask before sending the SOW

1. Have you performed work on a military installation? MacDill, Tampa, or any DoD site.
2. Are you NADCA certified, and do you have a certified ASCS who would be on site?
3. Are you registered in SAM.gov, and do you have a CAGE code?
4. What are your general liability limits, and can you name additional insureds?
5. Can your personnel pass base vetting, and are you willing to submit a full roster with identification to a Contracting Officer in advance?
6. Do you have capacity for six complete systems within a defined window during 0700 to 1600 weekdays?
7. Are you willing to work through us as the introducing party on a defined margin, or on a referral fee, whichever you prefer to structure?

Question 7 is the one that decides whether the conversation continues. Ask it
early. A contractor who wants the customer relationship directly is not a
partner on this.

## What to be straight about, on the call

We are the party that sourced the work. We are not the performing contractor
and will not present ourselves as one. The contractor signs, carries the
liability under section 5.2, designates the contract manager under section
4.4, and handles hazardous material removal under section 5.4. We handle the
customer relationship and take a margin on the price they give us.

Say that plainly. A contractor who discovers it later walks, and one who
hears it up front and stays is a partner worth having for the next one.

## What not to do

Do not give the contractor a target price, a budget, or the range we have
been working with internally. Their number has to be theirs. If we anchor
them, we lose the only independent check we have on whether the job is
priced correctly, and we will not know whether our margin is coming out of
our side or out of the quality of the work.
