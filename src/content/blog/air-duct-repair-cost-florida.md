Air duct repair is the service homeowners think about least until their energy bill climbs or certain rooms never cool down. The short answer on cost: it varies widely by scope — sealing a few leaks may cost a few hundred dollars, while resealing or replacing sections can reach into the thousands. In Florida's climate, the energy savings from sealed ducts often offset much of the expense. Here's how to think about it.

## Air duct repair cost ranges in Florida

Because "repair" covers everything from a small patch to replacing a duct run, pricing is best shown as ranges:

| Repair type | Typical Florida price |
| --- | --- |
| Sealing minor leaks / connections | $200–$600 |
| Resealing (mastic/aeroseal, whole system) | $700–$2,500 |
| Replacing a duct section | $500–$2,000 |
| Full duct replacement (system) | $2,000–$6,000+ |
| Insulation repair on ducts | $300–$1,200 |

These are illustrative Florida ranges. The real number depends on how much ductwork is affected and how accessible it is — attic work in Florida heat adds labor.

## Signs your ducts need repair

- **Uneven cooling.** Some rooms never get cold while others are fine.
- **High energy bills.** Cooled air leaking into the attic makes your AC run longer.
- **Weak airflow** at certain vents.
- **Visible damage** — crushed, disconnected, or torn ducts, often flex duct in attics.
- **Excess dust** entering through unsealed gaps.
- **Mold or moisture** on or inside ducts, common in humid Florida.

## Repair or replace?

This is the key decision:

**Repair when:**
- Damage is localized to a few connections or one run.
- The ductwork is otherwise in good condition and reasonably new.
- Leaks are the main issue and can be sealed.

**Replace when:**
- Ducts are old, brittle, or crushed in multiple places.
- There's heavy mold contamination throughout — repeated cleaning won't fix compromised material.
- The system leaks so broadly that patching becomes an endless expense.

A good contractor will inspect and tell you honestly which makes financial sense, rather than defaulting to the most expensive option.

## Why sealing pays off in Florida

Florida runs air conditioning nearly year-round, so every leak means paying to cool your attic instead of your home. Sealing ductwork:

- Lowers cooling costs by keeping conditioned air in the system
- Improves comfort by balancing airflow between rooms
- Reduces dust and allergens pulled in through gaps
- Eases strain on your AC, extending its life

For homes in [Miami](/service-areas/miami) and across the state, sealed ducts can meaningfully cut summer bills — which is why the upfront cost often pays back over time.

## Don't overlook mold

In Florida, duct damage and moisture often go together. If an inspection reveals mold inside compromised ductwork, sealing alone won't solve it — you may need proper [mold remediation](/services/mold-remediation) alongside repair or replacement. Address the moisture source, not just the symptom.

## Getting an accurate quote

- Insist on an **inspection** before any quote — repair pricing can't be guessed over the phone.
- Ask whether they recommend **repair or replacement, and why.**
- Get the **scope and price in writing.**
- Ask about **sealing methods** (mastic sealant versus internal aerosol sealing).

## The bottom line

Air duct repair costs range from a few hundred dollars for sealing to several thousand for replacement, and in Florida the energy savings frequently justify the investment.
