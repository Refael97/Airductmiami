Dryer vent cleaning is one of the least expensive and most worthwhile services a Florida homeowner can schedule. The short answer: it typically costs $100 to $200, and it directly reduces the risk of a lint fire while making your dryer run more efficiently. Here's the full 2026 pricing picture.

## Dryer vent cleaning cost in Florida

| Situation | Typical Florida price |
| --- | --- |
| Standard dryer vent cleaning | $100 to $200 |
| Long or complex vent run | $150 to $250 |
| Rooftop or second-story termination | $175 to $275 |
| Severe clog / bird nest removal | $200 to $300+ |
| Bundled with air duct cleaning | Often discounted |

Most single-family Florida homes with a straightforward vent run land in the $100 to $200 range. The price climbs when the vent is long, exits through the roof, or is severely blocked.

## What affects the price

### 1. Length and routing of the vent
A short vent through an exterior wall is quick. A long run that travels through the attic or up to the roof takes more time and equipment.

### 2. Termination point
Rooftop vents, common in some Florida homes, require safe roof access and cost more than a ground-level wall vent.

### 3. Severity of buildup
A vent cleaned yearly clears quickly. One neglected for years, or blocked by a bird nest, takes longer.

### 4. Bundling
Booking dryer vent cleaning alongside [air duct cleaning](/services/air-duct-cleaning/) often earns a discount, since the crew is already on-site.

## Why this cheap service matters so much

Lint is extremely flammable, and a clogged dryer vent is one of the leading causes of home dryer fires. Beyond safety, a blocked vent forces your dryer to work harder, which:

- Lengthens drying time
- Raises your energy bill
- Shortens the life of the appliance
- Can cause the dryer to overheat

In other words, a $100 to $200 cleaning often pays for itself through efficiency alone, before you even count the fire-safety benefit.

## Signs your dryer vent needs cleaning

- Clothes take more than one cycle to dry
- The dryer or laundry room feels unusually hot
- You notice a burning or musty smell during drying
- The exterior vent flap barely opens or is clogged with lint
- It's been over a year since the last cleaning

## The Florida angle

Florida's humidity means dryers often run frequently, and lint accumulates steadily. Homes in coastal areas like [Fort Lauderdale](/service-areas/fort-lauderdale/) may also see debris or nesting in exterior vents. Regular cleaning keeps everything flowing safely.

## Avoid the lowball trap

As with duct cleaning, be wary of prices that seem too good to be true. A genuine dryer vent cleaning involves disconnecting the dryer, running a brush and vacuum through the full vent length, and confirming airflow at the exterior. A crew that only vacuums the visible opening for $20 isn't clearing the fire hazard deeper in the line.

## The bottom line

At $100 to $200, professional dryer vent cleaning is inexpensive insurance against fire and wasted energy. Once a year is right for most Florida households.
